THE JOURNEY

FOUR DAYS.
ROOM TO BREATHE.

AUT → UPPER RAILA → UPPER NEAHI → SHANGARH → AUT
DAY 01

AUT → UPPER RAILA

Village Hike & Twin Towers of Raila

  • Pick-up from Aut.
  • Transfer to The Rabbit House Homestay, Upper Raila.
  • Check-in and lunch at the homestay.
  • Afternoon guided village hike through traditional surroundings, farms and village paths.
  • Explore the Twin Towers of Raila / Dhalyara Kothi, traditional architecture and Himalayan views.
  • Return to The Rabbit House, dinner, bonfire and overnight stay.
DAY 02

VILLAGE HIKE & BHATKANDHA SUNSET

Sunrise, Village Life & Golden Hour

  • Optional early-morning walk toward Sunrise Point for the mountain atmosphere and morning views.
  • Meet the three friendly furry companions around the homestay.
  • Breakfast, then a guided village hike through traditional houses, farms, forest paths and local surroundings.
  • Discover local lifestyle and culture, followed by lunch.
  • 4×4 drive to Bhatkandha Sunset Point, then hike to Bhatkandha Meadow.
  • Enjoy sunset and panoramic views; return by 4×4.
  • Dinner and overnight stay at The Rabbit House.
DAY 03

UPPER RAILA → UPPER NEAHI

Waterfall, School & Lake Experience

  • Breakfast and check-out.
  • Scenic drive to Sainj Fernway, Upper Neahi.
  • Stop at Rupiraila Waterfall along the way.
  • Check-in at Sainj Fernway and explore the nearby surroundings.
  • Visit a beautiful government school and Pundrikrishi Lake.
  • Return for dinner, leisure time and overnight stay at Sainj Fernway.
DAY 04

UPPER NEAHI → SHANGARH → AUT

Meadows, Waterfall & Shangarh

  • Breakfast and check-out.
  • 4×4 drive from Upper Neahi to Shangarh.
  • Explore Shangarh village and its meadow.
  • Visit Shangarh Waterfall and enjoy time for photography and leisure.
  • Lunch at a local café.
  • Evening departure and drop from Shangarh to Aut. Tour concludes.
